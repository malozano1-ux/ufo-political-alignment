# UFO Sightings and Political Alignment in the U.S.

Statistical analysis of UFO sighting records from the National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC), investigating whether state political alignment is associated with differences in sighting frequency and narrative characteristics.

## Overview

- **Course:** Stats — UCLA
- **Tools:** R (hypothesis testing, Poisson regression, text analysis)
- **Data:** National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC)
- **Date:** December 2025

## Research Question

*Do Democratic- and Republican-leaning states differ in UFO sighting frequency or in the narrative characteristics of sighting reports?*

## Methods

- Classified states by political alignment (Democrat- vs. Republican-leaning)
- Compared sighting rates across political groups using:
  - **Mann–Whitney U test** (non-parametric comparison of sighting counts)
  - **Chi-square test** (categorical sighting characteristics)
  - **Poisson regression** (modeling sighting frequency)
- Analyzed narrative descriptions for differences in reported shape, duration, and language across political groups

## Key Findings

- **Democratic-leaning states reported higher average UFO sightings** — a pattern supported consistently across Mann–Whitney, chi-square, and Poisson regression
- **Sighting characteristics (shape, duration, narrative language) were similar** across political groups, suggesting reporting behavior rather than perceptual differences drives the gap
- Results point to cultural or social reporting differences between political groups rather than actual differences in unexplained phenomena
